# IQAC Analyzer — Staff Instructions

1. Open the IQAC Analyzer link.
2. Upload one or more **complete IQAC activity reports** (PDF or Word).
3. Click **Analyze Reports**.
4. Wait for the analysis to finish.
5. Review the extracted activity rows carefully.
6. Correct any value that is wrong or incomplete.
7. Check the **Evidence Review** table, especially Proposal, Notice, Attendance, Event Report, Photos, Feedback and other evidence.
8. A missing Programme Table or another supporting document does **not** automatically invalidate the activity.
9. Click **Download IQAC_Master_Data.xlsx**.
10. Share/store the latest Excel through the college's approved IQAC workflow.

### Important

- Do not upload documents you are not authorized to send to a third-party AI service.
- The AI output must be human-verified before it is used for official IQAC/NAAC documentation.
- The app does not calculate an official NAAC score.
